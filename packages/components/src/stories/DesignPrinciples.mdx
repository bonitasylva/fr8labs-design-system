      <li><article><span aria-hidden="true">02</span><h3>Status first</h3><p>State the current shipment, invoice, document, approval, or exception status in text—not color alone.</p></article></li>
      <li><article><span aria-hidden="true">03</span><h3>Audit-friendly</h3><p>Keep identifiers, owners, timestamps, and state changes beside the decision they explain.</p></article></li>

      <li><article><span aria-hidden="true">06</span><h3>Accessible by default</h3><p>Provide persistent labels, visible focus, sufficient contrast, keyboard paths, readable text, and accessible names.</p></article></li>
      <li><article><span aria-hidden="true">07</span><h3>Component decisions serve workflows</h3><p>Choose components for the shipment, accounting, document, or approval task they clarify—not because they look modern.</p></article></li>
      <li><article><span aria-hidden="true">08</span><h3>Reference, do not copy</h3><p>Use legacy screens and Figma files to understand business behaviour, not to define the FDS visual foundation.</p></article></li>
      <li><article><span aria-hidden="true">09</span><h3>Stable Fr8Labs contracts</h3><p>Build with published FDS components, icons, and tokens—not the internal primitives behind them.</p></article></li>
      <li><article><span aria-hidden="true">10</span><h3>Reuse before variation</h3><p>Start with an approved component or composition. Create an exception only when workflow evidence justifies it.</p></article></li>
